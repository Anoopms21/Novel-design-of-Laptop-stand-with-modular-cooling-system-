# Novel Design of a Laptop Stand with Modular Cooling System

### Mechanical Product Design | DFM/DFA | Thermal Management

**University of Colorado Boulder | Design for Manufacturability – Project 2**

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the design and development of a laptop stand with an integrated modular cooling system, combining mechanical functionality, ergonomics, and thermal management.

The design explores how Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFM/DFA) principles can be applied to develop a practical, manufacturable, and modular product that supports laptop positioning and cooling.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a functional laptop stand with modular cooling integration.
- Improve laptop positioning and airflow around the device.
- Apply DFM/DFA principles to simplify product architecture.
- Evaluate material selection and manufacturing feasibility.
- Develop a modular design that supports assembly and maintenance.

| Feature | Design Intent |
|---|---|

## Design for Manufacturing & Assembly

DFM/DFA principles were incorporated into the product development process to evaluate manufacturing feasibility and assembly efficiency.

| Engineering Consideration | Design Approach |
|---|---|
| Part Design | Simplified component geometry |
| Material Selection | Balance of strength, weight, and manufacturability |
| Manufacturing Processes | Evaluation of suitable production methods |
| Assembly Design | Accessible and practical component interfaces |
| Modularity | Independent component assembly and replacement |
| Product Maintenance | Accessibility of cooling components |
